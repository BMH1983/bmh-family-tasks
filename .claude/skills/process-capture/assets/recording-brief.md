# Before you go: record your handover (about 2 hours)

We'd rather have your knowledge than a perfect goodbye. Please record yourself
doing your regular tasks — screen recordings with you talking out loud. Nobody
is judging the recordings; they get turned into written how-to guides after
you leave. Messy and narrated beats polished and silent, every time.

## Step 1 — List your tasks (10 minutes)

Write a quick list of everything you do: daily, weekly, monthly, and the rare
stuff. Don't polish it. This list comes with the recordings.

If you run out of time, record in this order:
1. Tasks only YOU know how to do
2. Anything involving money (invoicing, payroll, payments, banking)
3. Everything else

## Step 2 — Pick a recorder (whatever is already on your computer)

- **Windows:** open **Snipping Tool** from the Start menu and switch it to
  screen *recording* mode, or press **Win+G** for Game Bar and use its record
  button. **Check the microphone is turned ON** before you start.
- **Mac:** press **Shift+Cmd+5**, choose record, and turn the microphone on
  under Options.
- **Easiest on any computer:** start a Zoom or Teams meeting with just
  yourself, share your screen, and hit record. Bonus: these often create an
  automatic transcript — keep it with the video.

Do a 20-second test first and play it back: can you hear your voice? The
narration is half the value.

## Step 3 — Record (one task per video, 2–10 minutes each)

- Do the task **for real**, with a real example — not a description of it.
- **Talk the whole time.** Say what you're clicking, why, and the traps:
  "if this screen comes up, do this instead", "this has to be done before
  Wednesday", "so-and-so always needs a copy".
- **Show where things live:** the folders, bookmarks, and which login you use.
- **Never show or type a password on screen.** Say where passwords are kept
  instead ("it's in the shared password manager under X").
- Mistakes are fine — fixing a mistake on camera is gold, because it shows
  the next person what to do when it goes wrong.

## Step 4 — Hand it over (5 minutes)

- Name each file after the task: `weekly-payroll.mp4`, `new-client-setup.mp4`.
- Put all the videos, your task list, and any auto-transcripts in one folder
  on the shared drive (or a USB/cloud link).

That's it. Thank you — this genuinely matters.
